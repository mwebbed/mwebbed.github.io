Privacy Policy

Kids Art Scan Sync is a personal-use script run only by its owner, in the owner's own Google account.

  • It reads image and PDF files from designated folders in the owner's Google Drive.
  • It uploads those images to an album it created in the owner's Google Photos library.
  • It sends error notifications by email to the owner.

No data is collected from, sold to, or shared with anyone else. No data leaves the owner's Google account.
